Learning curve and threshold

The study path of TUTORIAL.md (chapters 1–49) and MANUALE.md is divided into 17 phases. For each phase, the difficulty (0–100) and the amount that can be practiced today with the runtime are estimated available. The curve shows how much of the language you master as you accumulate effort.

The threshold

Until the end of Triad-Core (stage S8, chapter 17 of the tutorial) theory and practice rise together: every example runs with node seed/dev/triad_core.cjs and the 37 Core conformance cases are green. Chapter 18 begins Triad-Full: the difficulty rises suddenly and the practice drops to 8%, because in the Full conformance only error (1/12). From there the theoretical curve continues to rise, the real one flattens: it is the Triad's learning threshold today.

The phases

Hover the points on the graphs for details of each phase.

Method

  • real masteryi = Σj≤i difficultyj × exercisablej / Σ difficulty; theoreticali = Σj≤i difficultyj / Σ difficulty (phases S0–S14, those of language).
  • Difficulty is a judgment (newness, traps, volume, prerequisites, tooling); the exercisable share reflects the status of conformance: Core 37/37, Full 1/12, Linux seed at E0 stage.
  • The order of the phases follows the chapters of TUTORIAL.md; the Manual column indicates the chapters corresponding to MANUALE.md, where Core and Full are presented on the same level.
