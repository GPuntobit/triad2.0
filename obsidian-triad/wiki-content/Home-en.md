# The Book of Triad

*Learn the Triad language and the [triad2.0](https://github.com/GPuntobit/triad2.0) project code, from first programs to the machine code seed.*

A book in four parts, to be read in order: each chapter explains the why of things, shows its use with commented examples (those of Triad-Core are executed and their output is faithfully reported) and closes with a summary and exercises. At the end, appendices for reference.
